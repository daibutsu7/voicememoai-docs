VoiceMemoAI (the "App") respects your privacy and is committed to the appropriate handling of personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it may be shared with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1-1. Information You Provide Directly

Type of Information Description
Audio data Audio files recorded within the App
Text data Transcription results and AI note content
Settings information App settings such as templates, dictionary entries, and naming rules

1-2. Information Collected Automatically

The App may collect diagnostic information such as crash reports and usage statistics. This information does not identify you personally.

1-3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personally identifying information such as name, address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts

2. Purpose of Use

Collected information is used only for the following purposes.

  • Transcribing audio (processed on-device)
  • Automatically generating AI notes (only text data is processed via an external API)
  • Syncing files to a cloud service (if configured by the user)
  • Improving the App and fixing bugs

3. Where Data Is Stored

Data Storage Location
Recorded audio files On the user's device only
Transcription text On the user's device only
AI notes On the user's device (also saved to the relevant service if cloud sync is configured)
App settings On the user's device only

5. Microphone Access

The App requires microphone access to record audio. Microphone access is used only while recording. You can change this permission at any time from the Settings app.

Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone

6. Data Retention

You can configure how long recordings, transcripts, and AI notes are retained from within the App's settings screen. You can delete your data from within the App at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from users under 13.

This Policy may be updated without prior notice. Material changes will be communicated through an app update or on this page.
